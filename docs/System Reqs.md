# After Playlist minimum system requirements.

Operating System: Windows 11
Adobe Software: 64-bit Adobe After Effects
Processor: Intel 6th gen or newer, or AMD Ryzen 1000 Series or newer.
Memory: 16 GB of RAM. That is the minimum to run After Effects.
Graphics: A compatible GPU with at least 4GB of GPU memory.
Storage: At least 8GB of available disk space. Recommended to have more than that though.
Spotify: The latest version of the Spotify desktop app for Windows.
